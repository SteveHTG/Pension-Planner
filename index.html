About

4350 Pension Planner gives estimates based on the plan rules as written. Official calculations come from the Plan Administrator, Resource Centers, LLC, at 561-624-3277. Where this app and the Plan document disagree, the Plan governs.

Not included yet: optional payment form factors (joint and survivor, life only), early retirement reductions, and service before October 2002.

How to use the Pension Planner

Five minutes, and you'll have your pension and DROP numbers.

  1. Enter your dates. On the Earnings tab, put in your hire date and the date you plan to enter the DROP or retire. The app works out your credited service.
  2. Upload the last paycheck of each calendar year. Tap Pay stub on a year, then Choose pay stub PDF and pick the stub from the payroll portal. Use the final stub dated in December so the YTD column covers the whole year. It is read on your device and never uploaded anywhere. Photos and screenshots don't work yet, only the PDF. After it reads the stub, the year and total fill in and you'll see what it found.
  3. Project the years you haven't worked yet. Scroll down to Project future years. It starts from a real year, applies the contract raises (2026 +4%, 2027 +4%), then your assumed raise after that. Incentive pay stays flat because the contract says incentives don't get raises. Tap Fill future years.
  4. Add every year you can. The plan averages your 5 highest years out of the last 10. Use Add another year for up to 10 years and the app picks the best 5 for you. Counted years get a red number; the rest are tagged "Not in your top 5."
  5. Read your results. Pension shows your monthly benefit. DROP shows your account growing year by year, and you can tap any year to see each month. Compare puts scenarios side by side. Export PDF saves it all in one report.
Good to know
  • Only 300 overtime hours a year count toward your pension. The app estimates your hours from OT pay and removes anything over the cap.
  • At 22 years your benefit jumps to 75%, then grows 2% a year, up to 100%.
  • Pension contributions stop while you're in the DROP, so your take-home goes up.
  • Everything you enter is saved only on this device. Clear it anytime in Settings.
  • These are estimates. Your official numbers come from the Plan Administrator, Resource Centers, at 561-624-3277.
